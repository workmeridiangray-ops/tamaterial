Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

TechAssures Lab ("the app") is a laboratory management app for material-testing labs, operated by TechAssures, Pune, India. This page explains what we collect and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and Google account ID when you sign in with Google, or your login name when you use a lab PIN.
  • Lab records you or your team enter: clients, projects, samples, test results, photos, invoices, payments, quotations and work orders.
  • Location (if you allow it) and timestamps recorded when samples are received, tested, approved or sent.
  • Sign-in records: device type, IP address and approximate country, shown to your lab administrator for security.

How we use it

Only to run the app for your lab: sign you in, store and show your lab's records, send in-app notifications and keep an audit trail. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Each lab's data is separate. Only people in your lab, according to their role, can see it. Our hosting and database providers store the data on our behalf.

Google sign-in

We receive only your basic profile (name, email). We do not access your Gmail, Drive or other Google data.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your lab uses the app. To have your data or account deleted, contact us at the email below.
